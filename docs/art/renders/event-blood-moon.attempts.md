## Attempt 1 · 2026-11-08 · REJECTED

Artist: Codex's built-in image tool (ChatGPT sign-in), called by the designer
through `codex exec` — the first plate drawn that way, as a test of the route.
Wording: the `--render` prompt from `node tools/mint-request.mjs EVT-36 --render`,
with the negative list appended as "Leave out: …".

What came back: 1536 x 1024, A4 landscape 3:2, clears the 866 px floor and the
1299 px want. The house style held — nib line, feathered hatching, thin washes over
the ink, slate night, ochre byre timber, the plank across the byre door, tracks
crossing the road, the gate shut and lashed.

**Rejected for one reason from the checklist:** the moon has faint rays round it,
and the brief bans exactly that ("no rays and no halo"; "no glow of any kind").

Also short of the brief, and would have been the next reason: the horizon sits at
about 45% down the page, not in the bottom third.

**Not the artist's fault — a fault in the brief.** The subject asks for stock
crowded into the corner of a walled field and, two sentences later, says "No
creature is in the picture". The plate drew the cattle and sheep, which is a fair
reading of the first sentence. One of those two sentences has to go before the
next attempt; until then any plate fails one of them.
