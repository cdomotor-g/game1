## What the brief asks for, and what arrived

The brief: *a fishing rod and its tackle laid at an angle across a bench* — a rod
in **three spliced lengths** with the joins whipped and varnished, **four wire
line guides** at widening intervals, a **banded cork grip** worn into finger
hollows, a **permanent set** in the rod from being stored strung, and beside it a
flat wooden winder, a **shallow horn box** open beyond it holding a dozen more
hooks, spare line and **two split lead shot**, with one hook parked in the grip.

The plate: the flat wooden winder, wound full of pale line and lashed with a red
cord; a banded cork float; a lead weight; and one barbed hook on a run-out of
line. No rod, no guides, no whipped joins, no horn box.

## Why it was kept, and what the card itself says

The card is `TOL-06`, and it is called **Fishing Line**, not Fishing Rod. A brief
whose subject sentence leads on a rod and spends two thirds of its words there is
weighting the picture away from the thing the card names.

But the card's own story rail settles more of this than the name does:

> Made at the forge, which surprises people: the rod is a stick and the line is
> string, and what you are actually buying is **the hooks. A dozen of them in a
> horn box**, and the whole kit **rolls up small enough to go inside a pack**
> rather than on it.

So the brief was right to put the horn box in — the card says the box of hooks is
the purchase — and the plate leaves it out, showing one hook and no box. And the
brief was arguably wrong to lead on the rod, which the card itself dismisses as a
stick. The plate's compact wound kit is a better picture of *rolls up small enough
to go inside a pack* than a rod laid across a bench ever was.

That is the shape of it: **the plate loses the one thing the card calls the
purchase, and gains the one thing the card calls the point.** It was kept because
it reads instantly as a fishing line and it is in the house register, and because
the missing horn box is a smaller loss than a picture of the wrong object.

## What would settle it

Not this file on its own. Either the brief is rewritten around the winder **with
the horn box of a dozen hooks back in it** and the plate redrawn to that, or the
plate stands and the story rail's horn box goes unillustrated — which is allowed,
since a story rail is flavour and not a parts list.

The brief is untouched and still describes the rod, so deleting
`docs/art/renders/tool-fishing-line.png` puts `TOL-06` back at DRAW with its
commission unchanged.
