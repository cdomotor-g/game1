# The modifications — object studies (modification-spinnaker … modification-salamander-grate)

Eleven single-object studies, one per modification card
(`data/modifications.json`), in deck order. A modification is a **fitting**, and
the whole deck rests on one rule: **the fitting is drawn off the vehicle, not on
it.** A spinnaker drawn on a ship is a picture of a ship; a spinnaker drawn as a
bolt of cloth, a spar, a set of sheets and a snuffer, laid out on a loft floor,
is a picture of the thing you are buying. The vehicle deck already has seventeen
pictures of vehicles.

Where a fitting cannot be understood without the thing it fits — a ram bolted to
a stem, a hinge that has to show its pivot — draw **just that much of the
vehicle**, ghosted in lighter line, and nothing more.

The four **enchantments** in this deck are arcane subjects and take the arcane
rules in full, exactly like the talismans: the flat violet (`#6B4C7D`) printed
out of register, no glow, no sparkle, no light source. An enchantment has no
object of its own, so it is drawn as **the moment of binding** — the alchemist's
bench, the chalked mark on the timber, the mana crystal going in — and the violet
sits on the mark, offset, like a misprint.

Render at 2000 px square or better, single subject, centred.

## Shared preamble — paste ahead of every prompt below

```text
Fine pen-and-ink drawing on aged paper, finished by hand with thin
watercolour washes: a plate from an illustrated shipwright's catalogue
of fittings. Drawn with a nib - a fine, springy, varied line, only a
little heavier round the outside of a form than within it, thinning and
breaking where the light catches an edge. Never a thick uniform outline.

All tone is hatching: short feathered strokes laid along the form and
crossed over one another in the darks, fine and dense enough to read as
continuous shadow at arm's length and still be separate strokes close
up. Nearly every surface carries some. Bare paper is kept for the
brightest highlights only.

Thin translucent watercolour is washed OVER the finished ink, so the
hatching reads through the colour everywhere: it tints the drawing, it
never fills it. The washes are uneven - a little darker where they pool
against an edge, stopping short of the line as often as they cross it.
Muted and desaturated throughout, warm ochre, rust red, dusty
grey-green, cold slate blue. Paper is warm aged oatmeal, never white.
Ink is warm near-black, never pure black.

A single fitting, centred, three-quarter view, laid out on a workshop
floor or bench with a short ground line, drawn at the scale of a
pattern-book plate: every rivet, seam, lashing, thread and repair
legible. New or newly overhauled, ready to fit, never ruined and never
installed - this is the part before it goes on. The page is worn from
the workshop: creases, a grimy corner, faint foxing. The silhouette
reads clearly.

Strictly no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows, no soft airbrushed
shading, no lens effects. No text, no letters, no dimension figures, no
border rule.
```

## The framing block

Every prompt below already carries it. It is repeated here because a new one
needs it too — see [`../09-framing-and-composition.md`](../09-framing-and-composition.md).

```text
FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge.
The fitting occupies the middle horizontal third, centred left-to-right, with
every named part well inside the middle 84% of the page. Low ground line in
the bottom third. Clear, quiet page above.
```

## modification-spinnaker — Spinnaker *(rigging, MOD-01)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

An enormous lightweight sail laid out folded on a sail loft floor in a broad
soft heap, one clew pulled clear and stretched out so the cut of the cloth and
the doubled seams read. The spar it flies from lying alongside with its bell
fitting; a coil of sheet on each side; a snuffer sock gathered on its hoop.
Sailmaker's palm and beeswax on the boards. Wash: rust red on the cloth in
broad flat panels, ochre on the spar, flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The heap of cloth, the
stretched clew, the spar and both sheet coils inside the middle 84%.
```

## modification-sweep-rig — Sweep Rig *(rigging, MOD-02)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

One complete sweep, off the ship and laid on trestles: a long straight FIXED
upper spar, a HINGED lower spar beneath it, an iron gooseneck pivot joining
them at one end with the pin drawn out and lying beside it, and a tall
trapezoid of laced sailcloth between the two spars. Two crew handles bolted to
the lower spar. The lower spar is shown swung open, so the hinge angle is
unmistakable. Guy lines coiled at the pivot end. Wash: dusty grey-green on the
sailcloth, ochre on the spars, rust red on the ironwork, flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The gooseneck pivot, the
pin, both spars and the laced sail inside the middle 84%.
```

## modification-copper-sheathing — Copper Sheathing *(hull, MOD-03)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A stack of copper sheets on a harbour quay, the top few fanned out so the
overlap and the nail pattern along the edges read clearly; one sheet stood on
end against the stack showing its full size against a man's height. A keg of
copper nails spilled at the base, a shipwright's hammer and a pair of tin
snips. Behind, ghosted in lighter line, the bottom two strakes of a hull in a
dry dock with the first sheets already on and the rest of the run bare.
Wash: rust red on the copper, cold slate on the dock stone, flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The stack, the standing
sheet, the keg and the tools inside the middle 84%; the ghosted hull may run
to the margins.
```

## modification-iron-ram — Iron Ram *(hull, MOD-04)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A forged iron ram beak on a low timber cradle in a smithy yard, seen
three-quarter from the front so the blunt vertical blade and the broad
scarfing flanges both read. Heavy hatching for the mass; the forge scale still
on it; long scarf bolts laid out in a row alongside with their washers. A
sledgehammer leaning on the cradle for scale. Behind, ghosted in lighter line,
the bare stem of a ship in dock waiting for it. Wash: rust red on the iron,
ochre on the cradle timber, flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The ram, the cradle, the
bolt row and the sledgehammer inside the middle 84%.
```

## modification-compound-boiler — Compound Boiler *(powerplant, MOD-07)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A compound engine unit standing free on its bedplate in an erecting shop: two
cylinders of plainly DIFFERENT diameter side by side on one crankshaft, the
small high-pressure one and the large low-pressure one, joined by a stout
receiver pipe between them with a lagging band round it. Crossheads, guide
bars, connecting rods and a flywheel all drawn true and assembled. Lagging
strapped in brass bands; a pressure gauge on a standpipe; an oil can and a
long-spouted feeder on the bedplate. Wash: cold slate on the cylinders, ochre
on the brass bands, rust red on the bedplate, flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. Both cylinders, the
receiver pipe between them, the crankshaft and the flywheel inside the middle
84% - the two different diameters are the card, and they must both be in frame.
```

## modification-keelbound — Keelbound *(enchantment, water, MOD-08)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]  [ARCANE RULES APPLY - see the talisman brief]

A ship's keel timber laid on blocks in an empty building slip, before a single
plank is on her: one long baulk of oak, scarf joints visible, chalk marks and
a shipwright's ribband along it. An alchemist kneels at the midships scarf with
a shallow bowl and a stub of chalk, drawing a line of marks into the grain; a
mana crystal sits in a wooden cradle beside the bowl. Rain water standing in
the slip. The marks on the timber carry the flat violet spot colour (#6B4C7D)
printed OUT OF REGISTER - offset about a millimetre from the black line,
bleeding past it like a misprint. Nothing glows. Other wash: cold slate on the
standing water, ochre on the timber, flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The scarf, the chalk
marks, the alchemist's hands and the crystal inside the middle 84%; the ends of
the keel may run to the margins.
```

## modification-warded-hold — Warded Hold *(enchantment, earth, MOD-10)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]  [ARCANE RULES APPLY - see the talisman brief]

The inside of a small cargo hold, looking down a row of crates lashed under a
net. In the foreground one crate lid stands slightly proud, and a thief's
crowbar is jammed under it and plainly not moving - the bar bent, the lid not.
A palm-sized mark is cut into the end grain of every crate in the row, the same
mark each time. The marks carry the flat violet spot colour (#6B4C7D) printed
OUT OF REGISTER, offset from the black line, and only the marks do. Nothing
glows. Other wash: ochre on the crate timber, cold slate in the deck shadow,
flat.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The proud lid, the bent
crowbar and the nearest two marks inside the middle 84%; the row of crates may
run to the margins.
```

## modification-salamander-grate — Salamander Grate *(enchantment, fire, MOD-11)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]  [ARCANE RULES APPLY - see the talisman brief]

A heavy cast-iron firebox grate stood on end on a foundry floor, bars thick as
a wrist, the casting flash still on the edges. Underneath it, held in a cradle
of iron straps bolted to the frame, is a shape the size of a cat that is not
quite an animal and not quite a coal - drawn as fused slag with the suggestion
of limbs folded under it, and NOT drawn as a creature with a face. A fireman
stands well back with a long slice bar. Tongs and a cooling trough beside.
The bound shape and the straps round it carry the flat violet spot colour
(#6B4C7D) printed OUT OF REGISTER, offset from the black line - and rust red
sits flat between the grate bars for heat. Nothing glows; the heat is hatching
and flat colour only.

FRAMING. Square plate, whole page drawn edge to edge. The grate, the bound
shape in its cradle and the fireman's slice bar inside the middle 84%.
```
