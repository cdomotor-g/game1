# The peoples — A4 plates (people-human … people-orc)

Five full-page studies for the book, one per playable people. Each is a
**working portrait, not a hero pose**: the people shown doing what their rules
say they are good at, drawn like a specimen page from a travelling naturalist's
folio. Full length, one or two figures, tools of their trade around them.

Render at A4 portrait proportion, 4000 px on the long side or better. All type
is set later — never generated.

## Shared preamble — paste ahead of every prompt below

```text
Black ink line art on warm unbleached paper, in the style of a worn 1600s
printed almanac: heavy uneven woodcut-style outlines, interior shading built
only from hand-drawn hatching and cross-hatching, over-inked pooling where
lines meet at sharp angles, bare paper for the lit surfaces.

Flat muted spot colour sits UNDER the black line like a mis-registered
letterpress run - solid areas of colour with no blending. Restricted palette:
warm ochre, rust red, dusty grey-green, cold slate blue. Paper is warm
oatmeal, never white. Ink is warm near-black, never pure black.

A full-page folio plate: one full-length figure study filling the page, drawn
from life at middle distance, standing on a short ground line with the tools
and clutter of their work around their feet. The page itself is worn from
travel - soft creases, a thumbed corner, faint foxing stains at the edges -
but the drawing is careful and complete. Chunky exaggerated proportions with
a bold readable silhouette.

Strictly no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows, no soft airbrushed shading,
no lens effects. No text, no letters, no border rule.
```

## people-human — Humans

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A human builder, neither young nor old, sleeves rolled, standing beside a
half-raised timber house wall with a mallet over one shoulder and a coil of
line in the other hand. Sturdy everyday clothes with patched knees.
At their feet: a water jug, a plumb line, a stack of split shingles. The face
is capable and unremarkable - the point of humans is that there is no point.
Wash: warm ochre on the clothing and timber, flat.
```

## people-dwarf — Dwarves

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A dwarf miner, broad as a door, stood at a shored mine mouth with a pick over
one shoulder and a hand lamp raised in the other hand. Braided beard tucked
into a wide belt hung with wedges and a tamping bar; heavy leather apron worn
shiny. At their feet: an ore cart edge, a coil of fuse, a chalked tally slate.
The gaze goes past the viewer, reading the rock. Wash: cold slate blue on
apron and shadowed stone, flat.
```

## people-elf — Elves

```text
[PREAMBLE]

An elf weaver-forager, tall and spare, standing between a standing loom and a
gathering basket, folding a finished bolt of fine cloth over one arm. The
other hand holds a sprig of herb up to the light between two fingers,
appraising it. Practical field clothes with a botanist's satchel of little
drawers. At their feet: shears, hanks of yarn, bundled herbs. Wash: dusty
grey-green on cloth and herbs, flat.
```

## people-halfling — Halflings

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A halfling farmer, round and cheerful and strong, mid-stride under a shoulder
yoke carrying two overfull baskets - bread, apples, cheeses, eggs - with a
hay fork tucked improbably under one arm. Bare feet on a ground line strewn
with dropped crumbs and one interested chicken. Behind, a low wattle fence
and a prize pumpkin. Wash: warm ochre over the whole harvest, flat.
```

## people-orc — Orcs

```text
[PREAMBLE]

An orc labourer-raider, huge through the shoulders, driving a post into the
ground with a maul one-handed - the maul's iron head is split and lashed
together with cord, halfway through being worn out like every tool an orc
touches. War scars and work scars share the same arms. At their feet: a
second broken maul head, a whetstone, a joint of meat on a cloth. The
expression is patient, not savage: this is a person mid-shift. Wash: rust red
on the harness and the maul's lashings, flat.
```
