# The monsters — A4 plates (monster-cinder-wolf … monster-storm-roc)

Twelve full-page studies, one per monster card, in deck order (MON-01 … MON-12).
Drawn like plates from a **field bestiary**: the creature observed from a safe
distance and recorded honestly, not a battle scene. One creature per plate, in
its home ground, with one small mundane object for scale where noted.

The four elements keep the palette honest: fire subjects carry rust red, earth
dusty grey-green, water cold slate blue, air the near-neutral pale tints. No
glow anywhere, ever — a fire monster is drawn hot the way an engraver would
draw it: hatching, smoke, and heat-shimmer lines, never light effects.

Render at A4 portrait proportion, 4000 px on the long side or better.

## Shared preamble — paste ahead of every prompt below

```text
Black ink line art on warm unbleached paper, in the style of a worn 1600s
printed bestiary: heavy uneven woodcut-style outlines, interior shading built
only from hand-drawn hatching and cross-hatching, over-inked pooling where
lines meet at sharp angles, bare paper for the lit surfaces.

Flat muted spot colour sits UNDER the black line like a mis-registered
letterpress run - solid areas of colour with no blending. Restricted palette:
warm ochre, rust red, dusty grey-green, cold slate blue. Paper is warm
oatmeal, never white. Ink is warm near-black, never pure black.

A full-page bestiary plate: one creature filling the page, full length, drawn
from observation at a wary distance, standing in a strip of its own terrain on
a short ground line. The pose is characteristic behaviour, not attack. The
page is worn from travel - creases, a thumbed corner, faint foxing - but the
drawing is careful. Bold readable silhouette.

Strictly no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows, no soft airbrushed shading,
no lens effects, no magic particles. No text, no letters, no border rule.
```

## Negative prompt — for all twelve

```text
gradient, glow, bloom, lens flare, drop shadow, soft shading, airbrush, blur,
depth of field, neon, saturated colours, pure white background, pure black,
photorealistic, 3d render, octane, unreal engine, digital painting, oil
painting, concept art, anime, chibi, cute, cel shaded, sparkles, magic
particles, glowing eyes, glowing runes, fire glow, light rays, watermark,
signature, text, letters, logo, UI, frame border, battle scene, multiple
creatures, human figures
```

## monster-cinder-wolf — Cinder Wolf *(fire, MON-01)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A lean grey wolf, ribs prominent, mid-prowl across dry grass - and behind its
ribcage the hatching deepens to a banked-coal darkness, with thin trails of
drawn smoke rising off its spine. Where it has walked, the grass is rendered
as burnt stubble in a neat trail of rings. Scale object: a scorched shepherd's
crook abandoned on the ground line. Wash: rust red inside the ribcage and on
the burnt trail, flat, no glow.
```

## monster-ash-drake — Ash Drake *(fire, MON-02)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A wingless drake the size of an ox, low-slung like an eel crossed with a
crocodile, swimming half-submerged through loose scree - stones piling in a
bow-wave at its chest. Plated hide drawn as overlapping ash-grey scutes,
cracked at the joints where the hatching goes coal-dark. A thin line of smoke
from each nostril. Scale object: a miner's abandoned pick bent double.
Wash: rust red in the hide cracks, grey-ochre on the scree, flat.
```

## monster-forge-wight — Forge Wight *(fire, MON-03)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A figure the shape of a smith at work, but made entirely of riveted black
iron plates that no smith ever forged, kneeling in an old mine tunnel to mend
a broken cart rail with its bare hands. The seams between its plates are
hatched deep-dark with a heat that is drawn, never glowing. Its head is a
smith's hood with nothing visible inside. Around it: neat rows of mended
tools, laid out like offerings. Wash: rust red on the seams and the mended
iron, cold slate on the tunnel stone, flat.
```

## monster-barrow-troll — Barrow Troll *(earth, MON-04)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A massive troll seated half-out of a broken burial mound like a man getting
out of bed, turf and stone slabs shed from its shoulders. Its hide is drawn
as packed earth and root: grass grows in its elbow creases and a small thorn
bush from one shoulder. Ancient grave-goods - a torc, a broken sword - hang
tangled in its chest hair. The face is heavy, patient and very hungry.
Scale object: a toppled standing stone. Wash: dusty grey-green on the turf
and hide, ochre on the bared earth, flat.
```

## monster-stone-boar — Stone Boar *(earth, MON-05)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A boar the size of a plough-ox with a hide of rounded river-boulder lumps,
moss in the seams, rooting peaceably at the base of an oak - the furrow it
has turned is drawn deep and neat as plough-work. Small bright eye, tusks
like worn pick-heads. Scale object: an upturned foraging basket spilling
mushrooms beside the furrow. Wash: dusty grey-green moss over grey hide,
ochre in the turned earth, flat.
```

## monster-gravel-wyrm — Gravel Wyrm *(earth, MON-06)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A blind legless wyrm, thick as a barrel and long as a wagon train, breaching
out of a scree slope in an arch - head like a battering ram of plated shale,
no eyes at all, mouth a ring of grinding stones mid-crush around a boulder.
Its castings - neat cairns of crushed, sorted ore - trail down the slope
behind it. Scale object: a surveyor's tripod, flattened. Wash: cold slate on
the plates, ochre on the castings, flat.
```

## monster-mire-strangler — Mire Strangler *(water, MON-07)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A fen horror mostly implied: a dry, inviting path through black-water marsh,
and beneath the drawn ripples either side a tangle of pale root-like limbs
converging on it, one breaking the surface in a slow arch of many joints. At
the path's end, hung neatly on a dead willow branch: a traveller's hat and
satchel it has no use for. Wash: cold slate on the water, dull grey-green on
the reeds, the limbs bare paper, flat.
```

## monster-reef-serpent — Reef Serpent *(water, MON-08)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A great serpent coiled twice around a harbour marker-post in easy loops, head
level with a moored fishing boat's gunwale, accepting a fish laid on the
transom - the fisherman's arm just entering frame, offering it. Scales drawn
as overlapping engraved crescents, barnacles on the older coils. The sea is
ruled wave lines. Wash: cold slate blue on the sea and the deep coils, flat.
```

## monster-deepwater-maw — The Deepwater Maw *(water, MON-09, unique)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

Open sea drawn in ruled wave lines, a two-masted trader heeled hard over in
the foreground - and beneath it, filling the whole lower page, the Maw rising:
a mouth wider than the ship is long, ringed with baleen-like teeth drawn as
ranked portcullises, old anchors and a church bell tangled in the gum line.
Only the mouth and one vast eye are shown; the rest is hatched depth.
Wash: cold slate blue graded by hatch density only, never by tone, flat.
```

## monster-rime-harpy — Rime Harpy *(air, MON-10)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A gaunt bird-woman perched on a wind-carved ice cornice, wings half-folded,
feathers drawn as overlapping icicle shapes with frost hatching at the edges.
She is examining a stolen brass compass in one clawed foot with the intensity
of a magpie. Around the nest below: a hoard of buckles, coins, a spyglass, a
locket, all drawn small and precise. Wash: pale slate on the ice and
feathers, one ochre note on the hoard's brass, flat.
```

## monster-dust-devil — Dust Devil *(air, MON-11)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

A standing spiral of wind made visible only by what it carries: grit, straw,
a snatched hat, torn sacking, drawn as a tight column of hatched motion lines
from ground to head height, leaning with intent. In the spiral's core, faint
and easy to miss, the suggestion of a face in profile. It crosses a desert
track past a laden, buckling handcart. Wash: warm ochre in the carried dust
only; the wind itself is bare paper and line, flat.
```

## monster-storm-roc — Storm Roc *(air, MON-12)*

```text
[PREAMBLE]

An immense eagle mantling on a sea-cliff pinnacle, wings half-spread the full
width of the page, feathers drawn as ranked engraved barbs with storm-cloud
hatching massed behind and below it - the weather is arriving with the bird.
In one talon, held gently, a full-grown ram, looking resigned rather than
harmed. Far below at the cliff base, a tiny climber on a rope, mid-ascent,
lunch in a shoulder bag. Wash: cold slate on the storm and wing shadow,
flat.
```
