The player boards

One per player. The character card top left, the five tracks up the middle, four cards of kit on the right, and the round's phases under the character. One per playable people — and the only difference between them is the player colour in the border banding and the maker's mark.

The board is a workbench (docs/art/06-components.md): sawn timber ground, iron-strapped edges, the tracks routed into the surface. The working surface stays neutral - the player's colour appears in the border banding and the maker's mark and nowhere else - so that a player's own tokens read against it.

Generated by tools/build-board.mjs from data/playerboard.json and data/components.json — edit those, re-run the tool, and never these files. Printing this page gives one board per sheet of A4 at true size — set your printer to 100% and landscape and turn off "fit to page", and the 3 mm of bleed each board is drawn with runs off the edge of the paper, which is the only place bleed is any use.

The five tracks

TrackCounts toWhat walks it
HHEALTH15Down as the hero takes hits, up as they rest - 2 a round at an inn, 3 where there is a healer.
BBURDEN (kg)30 kgTotal the mass of everything the hero is wearing, wielding and carrying, and stand the token on the first mark at or above that total.
SSPEED (hexes)15 hexesSet it at the start of a leg to the speed in travel.json for the mode and the ground, then walk it down a rung per hex entered. Halve it for a night leg under a lantern; a torch buys one hex, two on a road.
DDAMAGE15The vehicle the player is running - the train, the ship, the wagon, the horse. Up as it is damaged, down as it is repaired: one box a round in any settlement of town rank or better, at 5 coin a box.
MMANA15Everything the hero can hold at once: what their body holds innately, plus every talisman in a slot. Slaying a monster fills it; casting spends it.
