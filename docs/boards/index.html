The player board

One per player, and every one identical. The character card top left, six tracks up the middle, four cards of kit on the right, and the round's phases under the character.

The board says nothing about who is playing it. Race, calling, health, strength and burden limits are all printed on the character card, which is the thing that changes between players; the board is the furniture that card sits in.

Generated by tools/build-board.mjs from data/playerboard.json and data/components.json — edit those, re-run the tool, and never these files. Print one copy per player: set your printer to 100% and landscape and turn off "fit to page", and the 3 mm of bleed the board is drawn with runs off the edge of the paper, which is the only place bleed is any use.

The 6 tracks

TrackRunsWhat walks it
HHEALTH0–14Down as the hero takes hits, up as they rest - 2 a round at an inn, 3 where there is a healer.
SSTRENGTH0–14Rarely. It is the one track that mostly sits still: set it from the character card at setup and move it only when something lends or takes strength.
BBURDEN (kg)0–14 kgTotal the mass of everything the hero is wearing, wielding and carrying, and stand the token on the first mark at or above that total.
PPACE (hexes)0–14 hexesSet it at the start of a leg to the speed in travel.json for the mode and the ground, then walk it down a rung per hex entered. Halve it for a night leg under a lantern; a torch buys one hex, two on a road.
DDAMAGE0–14The vehicle the player is running - the train, the ship, the wagon, the horse. Up as it is damaged, down as it is repaired: one box a round in any settlement of town rank or better, at 5 coin a box.
MMANA0–14Everything the hero can hold at once: what their body holds innately, plus every talisman in a slot. Slaying a monster fills it; casting spends it.
