The adventure deck cards

Generated by tools/build-cards.mjs from data/*.json and the accepted plates in docs/art/renders/ — edit those, re-run the tool, and never these files. Only cards whose plate has been accepted are rendered (still waiting on renders: MON-14, MOD-02, MOD-03, MOD-04, MOD-05, MOD-06, MOD-07, MOD-08, MOD-09, MOD-10, MOD-11). Each card is shown here with its 3 mm bleed; the print page lays them out on paper at their true 63 × 88 mm, cut lines and all.

The backs

One per deck, so a face-down stack is identifiable across the table. Each carries its deck's name mirrored across the card's horizontal centre line — a back with an up and a down is a back that gives the card away. Described in data/components.json under back, and generated with the fronts.
