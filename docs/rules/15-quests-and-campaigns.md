# Quests and campaigns

A quest is a card you may take or leave; a campaign is a story the whole table plays through in order. This chapter is how a quest reaches you and what accepting it means, and the rules that every campaign shares. Each campaign's own rules, board, cast and chapters are in its annex — Campaign I, Homer's Odyssey, is Annex III.

## Quests

{{fig:plate:tile-mill,plate:monster-stone-boar,plate:tile-well,tile:inn|The miller's debt, the boar of Bramblehold, Dry Wells, and the inn where a rumour costs 5 coin}}

There are {{count:quests.quests}} quests in the deck, from an errand with a wage to a five-stage campaign quest that shapes a game, and every one is in Annex II, *Quests*. A quest reaches you in one of two ways:

- an **omen** on a discovery roll, which draws the top card of the quest deck ([Discovery and encounters](11-discovery.md));
- a **rumour** at any inn: pay 5 coin and draw the top card ([The map and travel](08-the-map-and-travel.md)).

1. **Read the card aloud**, and accept or decline it on the spot. A declined card goes to the bottom of the deck for somebody else.
2. **An accepted quest sits face up beside your board** until it is done or given up. It takes no kit slot. A party carrying a quest the road's owner accepted pays that owner no toll.
3. **Do what the task says.** A delivery is done when the goods change hands at the place named; a fight is done when the thing named is resolved; a task with a limit is done inside the rounds it names, counted from the round you accepted it.
4. **Take the reward** printed on the card: coin, victory points, mana, an item, or a standing favour — free beds at one inn, a cheaper survey, a discount — that lasts as the card says.
5. **Giving up** is free, and the card leaves the game unless it says otherwise.

A **campaign quest** has stages, taken in order, each with its own task and its own reward; the card prints the stage names and what the whole thing pays, and the tasks are in Annex I, *The campaigns, stage by stage*. Some stage rewards stand for the rest of the game — a route surveyed, a pass proved — and the card says so.

{{fig:map:korvane-reach|wide|The Korvane Reach, whose settlements the quest cards name}}

Settlement names on the quest cards are the Korvane Reach's; a campaign board brings quest cards of its own or none.

## Campaign mode

{{fig:plate:character-chr-21|margin|Athena; a campaign says where the party is and what happens there}}

The game has two ways to be played, chosen at setup ([The game](01-the-game.md)). **Free play** is any board, every deck shuffled, and the three victory conditions. A **campaign** is one story chosen at setup: it names its board, deals its cast, and its cards are read in order rather than shuffled. The economy, the market, travel and battle run exactly as in free play; the campaign says where the party is and what happens there.

### Setting a campaign up

{{fig:card:CHR-09,card:CHR-19,card:MON-15,card:MON-17|A campaign's cast and monsters carry its mark beside the card code, to be found by the corner}}

1. Choose a campaign from the annexes. Set out the board it names.
2. Deal its cast as its annex says. Every character and monster a campaign brings is a card in its own deck, marked with the campaign's mark beside the card code, so it can be found by the corner; in free play those cards deal like any other.
3. Sort the campaign's cards by chapter and stack them face down, chapter 1 on top.
4. Everything else is set up as in free play and every other deck is shuffled — but the event deck is drawn only when a campaign card says so.
5. Do whatever else the campaign's own setup says: a spare board for a fleet, stores written on a ledger, a party stood at its first place.

### Playing one

{{fig:plate:monster-sirens|third|The Sirens; the dice decide the price of a chapter, never its outcome}}

- **Turn a card when the story reaches it.** The card before it says when.
- **Read the passage marked *read aloud* first**, then do what the card says under *play*. What the card costs is printed beside it.
- **A card never offers a fork the story did not take.** Where the story chose, the card says what was chosen and the table does the same. What the dice decide is the price — figures lost, health lost, rounds spent — never the outcome.
- **A chapter that goes wrong** — the hero at 0 health with the story unfinished, a ship lost that the story does not lose — is replayed from its first line, keeping whatever the party has already lost. The story has one ending.
- **When the chapter is done, turn the next.**

### Winning a campaign

{{fig:plate:character-chr-16|margin|Penelope; the story has one ending, and the table brings it home together}}

A campaign is won by the table together, as its own *victory* says: the last card turned and finished within the game's {{rules.victory.gameLengthRounds}} rounds. The three free-play conditions are not scored, and nobody scores against anybody. If the rounds run out with a card unturned, the story has not been brought home.
