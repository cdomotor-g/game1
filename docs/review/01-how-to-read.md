# How to read this addendum

Book I was read the way an opponent reads a contract: to find where a table would be stuck, where two chapters disagree, where the data says something the chapter does not, and where a legal line of play makes a mockery of the design. What survived that reading is here, one finding at a time, each with a fix that could be pasted in. This addendum prints with the rules or stays in the box, as the table prefers.

## What was reviewed

The fifteen chapters of Book I, read against the data every number in them is drawn from, the design notes at the back of this book, and the digital twin of the game — the one reader of the rules that has to finish a game every time it is asked. The reading was done on six lenses in turn: contradictions between chapters; chapters against the data; playability from the book alone; exploits; ambiguity and edge cases; and the *Open* notes themselves, to see whether each interim rule holds. Every finding was then argued against before it was allowed to stand, and the ones that did not survive are not printed.

## Severity

| Word | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Breaks play** | A table cannot proceed, or the game cannot be finished, as printed |
| **Wrong** | The chapter contradicts the data or another chapter |
| **Exploit** | A legal line of play that trivialises a system or the win |
| **Unclear** | Two reasonable tables would rule differently |
| **Nit** | A slip worth fixing and not worth arguing about |

## Status

Every finding carries one of three words.

- **Corrected.** The chapter was changed in this printing, and the finding stays here so a reader of the last printing knows what moved.
- **Proposed.** The fix needs a decision — a number chosen, a data file edited, a rule settled — and the proposal is written out in full in *Proposals*, at the end of this addendum, so it can be taken or refused without re-reading the argument.
- **Open.** The chapter already says the point is undecided and offers an interim rule. The finding says whether that interim rule holds up.

## The tally

Thirty-nine findings, numbered R-01 onward in the order they are printed and not in the order of their weight, point at eighteen proposals, P-01 onward. Read *What breaks play* first: those are the handful that decide whether a game can be finished from this book tonight. Everything else is a game that plays, played slightly differently at two tables.
